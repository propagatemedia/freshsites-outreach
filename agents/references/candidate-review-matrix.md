# FreshSites Candidate Review Matrix

Purpose: stop false positives where a site looks messy but already has strong conversion infrastructure.

A candidate is only worth demo/outreach if the current website is both visibly weak AND commercially underbuilt. Do not target sites just because they are ugly, cluttered, purple, old Wix, or visually awkward.

## Core rule

**Ugliness is not enough.**

If a site already has prominent call routes, email, booking, forms, reviews, service pages, coverage areas, and proof, it is not a strong FreshSites target unless the demo is clearly better on the same conversion assets.

## Candidate score - 100 points

Score ROOT homepage first, then scan key service/contact pages. Use live browser + screenshot.

### A. Conversion infrastructure - 35 pts
- 8 pts - phone visible above fold or sticky/clickable
- 6 pts - email visible or obvious contact route
- 6 pts - contact form or quote form works / is obvious
- 6 pts - booking/pay/quote CTA exists above fold
- 5 pts - sticky header/action bar with call/email/book actions
- 4 pts - clear next step repeated after hero

### B. Trust infrastructure - 25 pts
- 7 pts - reviews/testimonials visible or linked from homepage
- 5 pts - real vehicle/team/job photos or credible local imagery
- 4 pts - years established / family business / accreditations / guarantees
- 4 pts - full address/service area visible
- 3 pts - social proof links like Checkatrade/Google/Yell/Trustatrader
- 2 pts - clear trading identity and footer details

### C. Service clarity - 20 pts
- 6 pts - core services visible above or near fold
- 5 pts - separate service cards/pages for key services
- 4 pts - coverage areas listed clearly
- 3 pts - emergency vs routine routes separated
- 2 pts - useful FAQ/guidance content

### D. Visual/technical presentation - 20 pts
- 5 pts - modern/professional first impression
- 4 pts - readable typography/hierarchy
- 4 pts - no broken/blank sections or severe layout defects
- 3 pts - responsive/mobile plausible
- 2 pts - good image quality
- 2 pts - no intrusive widgets/popups blocking action

## Decision bands

- **0-34: Strong target** - weak infrastructure and weak presentation. Build/send if email-ready.
- **35-49: Possible target** - needs multi-reviewer agreement and softer email. Do not use harsh score-led copy.
- **50-64: Usually skip** - only proceed if there is a severe defect directly hurting conversion and demo fixes it.
- **65+: Hard skip** - already commercially functional. Do not pitch.

## Automatic hard-skip / override rules

Hard skip unless two reviewers and the arbiter explicitly override:

1. Site has **4+ conversion assets** from A: sticky phone/header, email, form, booking/pay/quote CTA, repeated call route.
2. Site has **3+ trust assets** from B: reviews, real photos, years/accreditations, address/service area, third-party proof.
3. Site has separate service pages/cards AND clear areas covered.
4. The only major issue is visual taste, clutter, colour choice, or dated design.

## Severe defect rule

A severe visual/technical defect can still qualify a site, but only if it blocks trust or action, not merely aesthetics.

Examples that may qualify:
- broken hero or page preventing visitor action
- contact form broken/unusable
- phone/email hidden or not clickable on mobile
- site loads as mostly blank/error for normal users
- no clear services despite being a service business

Examples that do NOT qualify alone:
- old Wix look
- ugly colours
- too much copy
- random call-to-action buttons
- chat widget annoyance
- busy navigation
- repeated service text, if the phone/book/form/reviews are still obvious

## Multi-reviewer process

For every borderline candidate or any site where the initial reviewer wants to score below 4:

1. **Conversion reviewer** - scores A + checks action routes. Bias: protect against targeting functional sites.
2. **Trust reviewer** - scores B + checks proof, reviews, real business signals. Bias: do not target credible businesses with merely dated sites.
3. **Design reviewer** - scores C/D + notes real visual friction. Bias: aesthetics matter, but only as one part of the score.
4. **Arbiter** - reconciles. The arbiter must explicitly answer:
   - What already works on the current site?
   - What is genuinely broken or commercially weak?
   - Would a reasonable owner feel the email is fair?
   - Is the demo better enough to justify contact?

## Send gate

Outreach is allowed only when:

- candidate score < 50, OR severe defect rule passes with evidence
- at least 2 of 3 reviewers say `send_ok=true`
- arbiter says `send_ok=true`
- verified public email exists
- demo improves the specific weaknesses, not just the design

## Email tone by band

- 0-34: can mention concrete gaps, but still avoid insulting the business.
- 35-49: soft pitch only - "I made a cleaner, phone-first demo". No score line.
- 50+: do not send.
